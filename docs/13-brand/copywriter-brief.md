# Arabic Copywriter Brief

**Status:** Draft
**Owner:** Content lead

---

## Goal

Native Arabic copy across the site, per-country tone. **No machine translation** for Arabic — Google demotes it and customers distrust it.

## Scope (Phase 1 + Phase 6)

### Phase 1 (KSA only, ar-sa)
- Homepage hero + value prop + CTAs
- 1 product PDP (full description, features, FAQ)
- 1 group sales page (telescopic — recommended starter)
- Standard pages: shipping, returns, warranty, contact, privacy, terms
- Account/order tracking strings
- Auto-reply templates (initial set, ar-sa)
- 30+ FAQ entries (ar-sa)

### Phase 6 (group + landing pages, multi-country)
- 4 group sales pages × 3 Arabic locales = 12 unique copies
- 9 SEO landing pages × 3 Arabic locales = 27 unique copies
- 6+ guides per Arabic locale
- WhatsApp templates per locale (60+)

## Tone per country

| Country | Register | Vocabulary cues | Example greeting |
|---|---|---|---|
| KSA | Formal premium Gulf MSA | Quality, warranty, villa, contractor | "أهلاً بك في [Brand]" |
| Egypt | Practical clear MSA, slight Egyptian touch in marketing | Price, COD, durability, storage | "أهلاً وسهلاً 👋" |
| Iraq | Direct trust-focused MSA | WhatsApp-first, real photos, COD | "حياك الله، نحن هنا للمساعدة" |

## Deliverables format

- Markdown files per page/section
- Reviewed by 2nd reader (other native Arabic speaker)
- Imported via translations CSV

## Phase 0 deliverable

- Copywriter contracted.
- First-deliverable date set.
- Style guide drafted (tone, terminology — e.g., what to call a "telescopic ladder" in each country).
