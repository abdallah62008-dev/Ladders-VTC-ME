# Photography & Video Brief

**Status:** Draft
**Owner:** Design lead + Ops lead

---

## Goal

Real product photography for PDPs (mandatory — never AI-generated for actual products). Lifestyle imagery for group sales pages, hero sections, and marketing.

## Shot list per SKU

1. **Hero shot** — clean white/concrete background, full product extended.
2. **Folded form** — collapsed/folded view emphasizing compactness.
3. **Detail shots** — anti-slip foot, top hook, locking mechanism, weld close-up.
4. **In-use shot** — person using ladder (back view OK to avoid likeness rights complications) for context.
5. **Storage shot** — folded ladder in garage / car trunk (proves "fits in car" claim).
6. **Dimensional drawing** — alongside photography or separately rendered.
7. **Video clip** — unfolding/closing, 5–8 seconds, muted (autoplay-on-scroll friendly).
8. **Safety video** (optional) — load-test demonstration if applicable.

## Lifestyle / mood (per group)

- Home / apartment scene
- Villa exterior / maintenance
- Contractor on site
- Warehouse use
- Electrician (fiberglass group only)

These scenes can use Freepik premium or commissioned photography. Real product composited in if the scene is generic.

## Requirements

- Resolution: minimum 4K (3840x2160), preferably medium-format.
- Format: TIFF master + DNG for raws.
- Color: D65 daylight; neutral white balance; minor color grade in post.
- Naming convention follows `13-brand` (TODO finalize).
- Licensing: full commercial transfer.
- Per-locale lifestyle: Saudi villa scene for KSA, Egyptian apartment for EG, Iraqi context for IQ.

## What NOT to shoot

- ❌ Models holding the product unless we have likeness release on file.
- ❌ Branded items (other brands) in shot.
- ❌ Anything resembling unsafe ladder use.
