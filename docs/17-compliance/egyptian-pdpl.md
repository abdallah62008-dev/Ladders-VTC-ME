# Egyptian PDPL Compliance

**Status:** Draft (stub)
**Owner:** Legal

Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020).

## Key obligations

- Consent for processing personal data.
- Customer rights: access, rectification, deletion.
- Data Protection Officer where thresholds met.
- Data breach notification.
- Cross-border transfer restrictions.

## Application impact

- Same controls as KSA PDPL plus Egypt-specific consent text.
- Marketing consent must be opt-in.
