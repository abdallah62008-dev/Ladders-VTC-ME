# On-Call Rotation

**Status:** Draft (stub)
**Owner:** Infra lead

---

## Roles

- **Engineering on-call** — handles Sev-1 / Sev-2 technical issues.
- **Ops on-call** — handles operational issues (logistics, COD, customer-facing).
- **Security on-call** — handles security incidents (breaches, leaks, abuse).

## Cadence

Weekly rotation per role. Hand-off Mondays.

## SLA

- Sev-1 acknowledgment: < 15 min.
- Sev-2 acknowledgment: < 1 h business hours; < 4 h off-hours.
